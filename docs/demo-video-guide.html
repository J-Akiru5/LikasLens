⚙️ Production Notes

Audio

Your track "FULL FORCE MINSET DOPAMINE" works well for energy. Layer ambient nature sounds during Part 1 for immersion. Add camera shutter SFX at 0:40 and notification ping at 1:00. VO should be confident, measured — journalistic tone, not salesy.

Text Overlay Style

Use a clean sans-serif font (Inter, SF Pro, or similar). White text with subtle drop shadow. Center or lower-third positioning. Fade in/out animation — minimal movement.

Transitions

Use simple cross-dissolves between sections. Avoid flashy transitions. The 1:10 → 1:15 brand card should fade to black. 2:47 → 2:50 should fade to white before the montage.
