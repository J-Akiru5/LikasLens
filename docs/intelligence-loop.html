Phase Breakdown · Four Pillars of Environmental Intelligence

Phase 01
Collect

Citizens capture real-time field imagery through the Mobile PWA. EXIF data is stripped client-side before upload, preserving Ghost Mode anonymity. Reports include GPS coordinates, timestamp, and optional notes.

Mobile PWA + camera capture
Client-side EXIF stripping
Offline queue via IndexedDB
GPS + timestamp tagging
Multi-image burst capture
Mobile PWA Next.js Ghost Mode
Phase 02
Analyze

Uploaded imagery is processed through the Neuro-Symbolic AI Pipeline. YOLOv8 Nano detects objects, a Gremlin graph traversal links violations to laws, and Gemini 2.5 Flash generates a human-readable triage summary.

YOLOv8 Nano computer vision
Waste + COCO dual-model detection
Gremlin graph (Neo4j) traversal
Gemini 2.5 Flash narrative
Severity score computation
YOLOv8 FastAPI Neo4j Gemini
Phase 03
Predict

Historical report clusters and severity scores feed a geographic hotspot model. Risk surfaces are rendered as heatmaps on the Admin Portal, letting LGUs and DENR identify emerging crisis zones before they escalate.

Geographic hotspot clustering
Historical trend analysis
Risk heatmap generation
Seasonal pattern detection
Prediction confidence scoring
PredictionService Laravel Heatmap
Phase 04
Act

Verified reports are routed to the correct LGU or national agency via the Admin Portal. SLA timers track resolution, blockchain records provide tamper-proof accountability, and resolved tickets feed back into the prediction model.

Automated LGU / agency routing
SLA timer enforcement
Blockchain audit trail
Resolution → feedback loop
Citizen reward & eco-credits
Admin Portal SLA Engine Blockchain
Why a loop and not a pipeline?

Most civic-tech platforms stop at Act — a report is filed, a ticket is closed, done. LikasLens treats resolution data as a new signal. Every closed ticket refines hotspot predictions. Every verified violation strengthens the YOLOv8 training feedback. Every LGU response time updates the SLA pressure model. The loop never stops — it learns, tightens, and gets sharper with every citizen who opens the camera.
