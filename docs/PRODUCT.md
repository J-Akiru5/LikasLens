## Users

**Primary — Citizen Reporter**
A person who has witnessed an environmental violation and decided to act. They are outdoors or mobile, using a phone. Emotional state during use: civic resolve — not panicking, acting. A subset face real physical risk (illegal logging near gang activity, industrial dumping with surveillance) and activate Ghost Mode. For these users the product is protective infrastructure, not just a reporting tool.

**Secondary — Public Accountability Viewer**
Anyone checking whether their government is resolving complaints. They browse the Public Scoreboard without a login. Emotional state: skepticism being tested. They are looking for evidence that accountability is real, not theatre.

**Tertiary — Admin / Analyst**
Government or NGO staff who triage, route, and resolve tickets. Desktop-primary. High volume, efficiency-driven. Emotional state: focused and task-driven. Every second the interface wastes is a second a report sits unactioned.

The Citizen Reporter gets the most design investment. Ghost Mode is their safety net and must be designed with that weight.

## Product Purpose

LikasLens routes environmental violation reports through AI image analysis to the correct government agency, tracks every resolution publicly, and protects whistleblowers via Ghost Mode. Success means: every report finds its destination, every government response (or non-response) lives on the public record, and every person who takes a risk to report feels the system has their back.

## Brand Personality

Voice: Precise and civic. Not bureaucratic. Not activist. Not alarmist. The platform speaks like a journalist with a long memory — authoritative without being cold, urgent without being sensational.

Three-word personality: **Vigilant. Accountable. Precise.**

Emotional goals:
- Citizen Reporter: *"I documented this. It's in the system. Someone is on it."*
- Public Viewer: *"The numbers don't lie. Either they fixed it or they didn't."*
- Admin: *"I can see everything I need to make a decision in the next 30 seconds."*

## Anti-references

- **Passive climate branding**: rounded corners, leaf illustrations, pastel greens, warm Instagram tones. That aesthetic signals aspiration. LikasLens signals action.
- **Generic SaaS dashboards**: Notion-like, Linear-like — neutral grays, weightless cards, "clean and modern" as a personality substitute. LikasLens has a specific identity. It is not a blank canvas.
- **Government emergency portals**: high-alert reds, heavy authority-weight type, blunt bureaucratic chrome. LikasLens is civic, not military.
- **Corporate ESG reports**: dark navy, gold highlights, polished but empty. This product is for citizens on the ground, not boardroom presentations.
- **Glassmorphism as a default**: blurred panels on blurred panels signal decoration over function. Every surface must be readable and purposeful.

## Design Principles

1. **Evidence, not aesthetics** — every design element must reinforce that this is a record-keeping system. Data is displayed with precision. Status is unmistakable at a glance, without labels.

2. **Civic trust through consistency** — the system earns credibility by behaving predictably. A status color that means something here means the same thing on every screen. A timestamp always reads the same way.

3. **Protection is a feature** — Ghost Mode is not a cosmetic dark theme. It is a safety protocol. Its activation must feel like entering a different operational context, not toggling a color scheme.

4. **Accountability is visible** — the Public Scoreboard must be impossible to dismiss. Numbers, resolution times, and status indicators are legible at a glance without having to read the page.

5. **Density serves the operator** — Admin screens earn the right to be dense. Whitespace is not a virtue when an analyst has 200 tickets to triage. Every element in the admin view earns its place by showing actionable information.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG AA minimum across all surfaces; AAA on critical paths (report submission, Ghost Mode activation, scoreboard data)
- All text/background combinations verified at minimum 4.5:1; large text and UI components at 3:1
- Color is never the sole status indicator — every semantic color is paired with an icon or label
- Ghost Mode must be usable in near-dark physical environments — bright screens attract attention when reporting in dangerous situations; consider dim-safe contrast levels
- All interactive elements are keyboard-navigable with a visible focus ring
- Report submission flow must be operable one-handed on a phone
- Reduced motion support required on all animations — this is a field tool, not a showcase
