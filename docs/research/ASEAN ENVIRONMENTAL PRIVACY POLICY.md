**`ASEAN ENVIRONMENTAL PRIVACY POLICY`**  
`The ASEAN environmental privacy policy is governed by the ASEAN Framework on Personal Data Protection and regional environmental mandates. It protects personal data collected by environmental bodies by ensuring transparency, secure storage, and strict limitations on third-party disclosure.` 

`Specific privacy and data handling rules within the regional environmental framework include:`

* `Information Collection: Environmental portals collect basic user information (e.g., name, email, usage data) to deliver public services and improve platform functionality, as outlined in the Privacy Policy - ASEAN Environment Knowledge Hub.`  
* `Data Security: Member states and environmental centers employ standard security technologies to safeguard your personal data against unauthorized access or breaches, in accordance with the Privacy Statement - ASMC - Regional Haze Situation. [1]`  
* `Data Disclosure: Personal data is strictly kept confidential and is not shared with third parties unless required to comply with applicable laws or with explicit consent.Environmental Data & Access: In alignment with the ASEAN Declaration on the Right to a Safe, Clean, Healthy and Sustainable Environment, procedural rights emphasize public access to environmental information. However, this access is balanced against the protection of sensitive personal or corporate data submitted via platforms like the ASEAN Simplified ESG Disclosure Guide.`
