### 6. Features Worth Adding from Competitors

**From LUWAS — Cross-barangay pattern escalation:**
If 5+ reports cluster within a 2km radius within 72 hours, automatically upgrade ticket severity to "systemic incident" and escalate to provincial level rather than just local LGU. This mimics LUWAS's Silent Area scoring intelligence and gives LikasLens a pattern-detection layer above individual reports.

**From Althena — Explain mode:**
When an LGU officer receives an incident ticket, they can tap "Explain" to see why the AI flagged this image, which rule was triggered, which law governs enforcement, and what the recommended action is. FIWARE's Explain layer is Althena's strongest feature. You can approximate this with your existing Cosmos Graph — just surface it in the UI.

**From CardinalMu — Confidence tier display:**
Show the LGU dashboard user a confidence tier badge (Watch / Advisory / Confirmed) rather than a raw score. Judges will immediately recognize this as a well-designed information architecture. Internally it maps to your existing confidence threshold, so the engineering cost is zero — it's purely a UI label.
